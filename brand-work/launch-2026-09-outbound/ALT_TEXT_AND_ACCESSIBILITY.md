## Historical Motion Transcript

This records existing motion copy, including its original uppercase styling. It has not been recertified by the static refresh. Keep the films held until the actual picture, audio, end cards, and accessible text are reviewed together.

All three motion cuts use music and a confirmation chime without spoken dialogue. On-screen sequence:

1. **FINDS THE LEAD.**
2. **MAKES THE CALL.**
3. **BOOKS THE NEXT STEP.**
4. **FIND. CALL. BOOK.**
5. **GET YOUR SALES BOT — BOTBOUND.ME**

There is no spoken dialogue or narration. Accessible delivery should still describe meaningful audio cues and provide the campaign message as readable platform text. Do not invent speech captions. A current caption or post does not correct retired words baked into an old film.

## Accessibility Notes

- Do not repeat the full visible headline in surrounding post copy and alt text unless the platform requires it.
- Mark purely atmospheric Runway source art as decorative when used behind accessible HTML text.
- The investor PDF contains selectable text; use the PDF rather than the PNG for document distribution.
- Keep the campaign CTA as real linked text outside raster creative whenever the placement supports it.
