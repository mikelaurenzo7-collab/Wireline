# BotBound — Investor One-Pager Source Copy

## Put your Bot on the line.

**BotBound is the operating layer for one trained Bot across calls, texts, and email.**

Customer demand arrives on its own schedule. The people best equipped to answer it are often doing the work: serving a customer, driving to the next job, making the product, or running the operation. A missed call and a delayed follow-up are usually not separate software problems. They are one broken conversation.

## The product

BotBound gives an owner one Bot with one identity, operating context, rule set, approval posture, and outcome record across voice, SMS, and email.

**Build -> Train -> Launch -> Improve**

- **Build** a focused Bot around a real job.
- **Train** it on facts, sources, rules, voice, tone, tools, and workflows.
- **Launch** it on the channels the owner enables.
- **Improve** it from real conversations, outcomes, approvals, and review.

The Bot can answer a call, continue the conversation by text or email, and leave the next action ready for review when the required tools, permissions, approvals, and handoffs are configured.

## Why this wedge matters

The phone is still where high-intent customer demand appears for many owner-led and service businesses. Yet the work itself makes picking up difficult. BotBound starts with that concrete second-ring problem, then preserves the conversation across the channels and operational context that usually fragment afterward.

The goal is not another generic chat window. It is a practical operating layer between incoming demand and the next responsible action.

## Product and infrastructure advantage

- **One live conversation runtime:** OpenAI Realtime powers browser and phone conversations.
- **Voice choice with runtime consistency:** users choose from ten built-in OpenAI Realtime voices; the selected voice stays stable through an active session.
- **One carrier layer:** Twilio supplies numbers, PSTN/SIP transport, and SMS.
- **Server-owned policy:** BotBound keeps tools, approvals, account state, compliance boundaries, and outcome persistence on the server.
- **Private rehearsal before live work:** Free Builder supports one draft Bot and bounded private simulation before paid activation.
- **Clear commercial boundary:** every active paid Bot receives a dedicated production number within plan capacity; live plans begin at $19/month; usage beyond included capacity stays prepaid rather than becoming an automatic surprise overage.

## Business model

BotBound combines a free private builder with recurring paid live plans.

- **Free Builder:** $0; one private draft Bot and private simulation.
- **Starter:** from $19/month for one live Bot and one dedicated production number.
- **Starter Plus:** $39/month for two live Bots in one workspace.
- **Pro:** $79/month for up to five specialized Bots with routing, approvals, and broader connected actions.
- **Scale:** $159/month for up to fifteen live Bots; intentionally absent from the current public self-serve catalog.

The pricing model makes voice capacity, carrier resources, flexible runtime, and managed AI usage explicit. It does not rely on hidden postpaid overages.

## Launch posture

BotBound is preparing a founder-led, product-proof launch. The company is using explicit demo and founder accounts to dogfood creation, training, live calls, text/email continuation, approvals, outcomes, billing, and the desktop/mobile experience before expanding acquisition.

The immediate objective is credible product proof and a small group of design partners — not manufactured scale. Customer logos, testimonials, traction, revenue, ROI, and performance metrics will be used only when they are real, permissioned, and documented.
