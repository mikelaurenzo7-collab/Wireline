# BotBound Brand System

Updated September 20, 2026. This reconciles the September Drive sales campaigns, September 19 incoming-call pilot focus, and the owner's latest direction: broad inbound/outbound clarity, minimal copy, Title Case, and no public setup sequence.

## Current Message

**Stay in the Work.**

Bots for calls, texts, and email.

General category: **Inbound & Outbound Bots**.

Brand close: **Put Your Bot on the Line.**

Broad companion: **Small Team. Bigger Reach.**

Primary CTA: **Start Free**.

Offer disclosure: **Private testing free. Live use paid.** Activation depends on availability, account eligibility, and configuration.

The near-term pilot is incoming calls with one configured, permitted follow-up. It is a limited pilot scope, not a limit of one Bot per customer.

## Preserved Sales Campaigns

[Your Next Sales Hire](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1KGRPCkQH1DLPURJOXTVp3BL1rCp0OgBO) and [Give Sales a Hand](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1j7Pmp_MrzkSNljwPH_MkyPeZ86GWltuR) remain sales-specific creative variants.

Use **AI Sales Bots** near **Meet Your Next Sales Hire.** so the software category is clear. “Sales hire” is a campaign metaphor, not a staffing service, human replacement, or staffing-equivalence claim. The craft-and-gripping-hand idea remains useful; the new broad direction does not discard it.

“Every Lead. A Next Step.” is a product ambition, not a guaranteed result. Deal memory, adaptive next-step plans, quote assistance, daily decision briefs, and complete sales-cycle autonomy remain proposed until shipped and verified. Sales positioning does not establish outbound availability.

## Identity

- Use the approved white/platinum operator, friendly violet eyes, black visor, antenna, and classic violet receiver.
- Keep the receiver at the robot's left ear with the white hand visibly gripping it. Do not redraw the robot or turn the receiver into a headset.
- Keep both B glyphs in **BotBound** complete. Never notch, stencil, split, crop, or replace them.
- Use the current polished operator for hero campaigns and the app icon for compact placements. The flatter operator remains a compact editorial alternative.
- Set the complete wordmark in Inter Tight ExtraBold, weight 800. Keep Bot platinum or obsidian and Bound Aurora Violet; do not gradient-fill it.
- Preserve at least one antenna-ball diameter around the icon and the lowercase-o height around the lockup. Minimum lockup width: 144 px on screen.

## Type and Color

Headlines use Inter Tight 800; body uses Inter. IBM Plex Mono serves compact operational labels. Author every title and headline in Title Case. Body copy uses sentence case. Reserve uppercase for short utility labels; never use CSS to recase names or customer text.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Canvas | Obsidian `#0A0A0E` |
| Deep canvas | Black Violet `#050508` |
| Primary type | Platinum White `#F5F4F8` |
| Signal | Aurora Violet `#A78BFA` |
| Receiver | Violet `#8B5CF6` |
| Light signal | Lilac `#C4B5FD` |
| Secondary type | Smoke `#B6B1C6` |

Keep most surfaces obsidian and platinum. Use violet to focus attention, not fill every space. Headlines are short and left aligned where the composition allows. Conversion layouts carry one CTA and a legible free/private versus paid/live disclosure.

## Art and Motion

Show competent people absorbed in specific work. Use warm practical light, deep shadows, restrained violet accents, and real negative space for copy. Generated scenes are advertising illustration, not customers, hardware demonstrations, or product proof.

Preserve the robot's face, antenna, receiver, and gripping hand. Avoid fake interface screens, fabricated transcripts, invented customer results, neon overload, and source-art text that cannot be corrected independently.

The Second Ring signal and two-note chime remain recognizable historical creative elements. Sales films and craft films retain their original source and audio records. A new brand document does not recertify their pixels or sound. Review each film at actual playback size before using it with new copy.
