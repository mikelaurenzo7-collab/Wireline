# Help: your own voice on calls

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Your Bot can answer phone calls in your own voice.

## Create it

1. Open your Bot, then **Voice**, then **Clone my voice**.
2. Confirm it is your voice, or that the speaker gave you written permission.
3. Read the on-screen script in a quiet room for about 90 seconds (10 seconds
   minimum, 2 minutes maximum). The meter shows your level as you go.
4. Review the quality check. If it suggests a retake, follow the tip and record
   again.
5. Compare your voice with the stock voice, then choose **Use this voice**.

Call your Bot's number to hear it live. **Switch back to stock voice** reverts in
one tap, and deleting a voice removes it everywhere.

## What to expect

- Cloned voices are used on phone calls. Browser rehearsals use the stock voice.
- Stock voices are tuned for the fastest replies, so they remain a good choice
  for very high volume lines.
- If your cloned voice is ever unavailable during a call, the call continues on
  the closest stock voice. Calls are never dropped for this reason.
- Each account can keep two cloned voices. Voice cloning is available in the
  United States, except Illinois, on eligible plans.
- BotBound never stores your recording. It keeps only a fingerprint, its length,
  and its quality score so you can manage the voice.
