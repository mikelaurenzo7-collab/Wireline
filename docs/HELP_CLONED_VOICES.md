# Help: Your Own Voice On Calls

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Your Bot can speak in your own voice.

## Create it

1. Open your Bot, then **Voice**, then **Clone Voice**.
2. Choose the state you live in, read the voice release, and sign it by typing
   your full legal name. Only you can clone your voice.
3. Read the on-screen script in a quiet room for about 90 seconds (10 seconds
   minimum, 2 minutes maximum). The meter shows your level as you go.
4. Review the quality check. If it suggests a retake, follow the tip and record
   again.
5. Compare your voice with the stock voice, then choose **Use This Voice**.

**Switch back to stock voice** reverts in one tap, and deleting a voice removes
it everywhere.

## Prepared Phone Voices

Open **More Phone Voices** to search and preview public Cartesia voices and
ElevenLabs' prepared voices. Selecting one saves it for that Bot's incoming and
outgoing calls. Selecting a clone or a built-in voice replaces that selection.
Your browser rehearsal continues to use its built-in voice.

This catalog excludes private workspace voices and ElevenLabs marketplace
voices with separate rates. It does not upload your recording to Cartesia.
Provider outages can trigger the same stock-speech fallback used by cloned
calls. A preview confirms sample playback; test a real call to assess telephone
quality and interruption behavior.

## What to expect

- Each voice card shows whether the voice is ready for live calls. A ready
  voice is used for incoming calls, outgoing calls, and verified owner phone
  tests. Make a test call after selecting it so you can hear the complete experience.
- You can interrupt naturally. Your Bot stops speaking and listens, then
  continues the conversation without repeating a tool action.
- Browser rehearsals use the stock voice, and the rehearsal screen says so.
- If cloned speech is unavailable during a call, BotBound attempts to continue
  in the stock voice. The comparison preview uses that same fallback voice.
  An outage affecting both speech services can still end a call.
- Each account can keep two cloned voices.
- Tip: you can switch any Bot back to a stock voice in one tap, anytime.
- Recording remains a separate, per-Bot choice. When enabled, its disclosure
  is spoken and the same recording continues if the voice changes to stock.

## Your recording and your privacy

- A voice provider that works for BotBound processes your recording to create
  the voice and keeps the voice model until it is deleted. BotBound does not
  keep the recording itself. It keeps the voice, a fingerprint of the file, its
  length, its quality score, and your signed release.
- The voice is used only for your Bot's calls and previews. It is never sold,
  leased, traded, or used to identify anyone.
- Retention schedule: a voice is permanently destroyed, in BotBound and at the
  voice provider, when you delete it, when you close your account, after 12
  months without being used, or 3 years after it was created, whichever comes
  first.
