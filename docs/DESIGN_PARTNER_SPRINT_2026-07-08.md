# Design Partner Sprint — First Five

Updated: 2026-08-11  
Status: **Active pre-customer founder motion**  
Authority: `docs/LAUNCH_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_2026-08-11.md`

## Goal

Recruit **3–5 design partners** with an obvious missed-call, inbound-response, scheduling, or follow-up problem and prove one useful workflow end to end within seven days.

> Build → Train → Launch → Improve.

The first partners are for evidence, not scale. Do not optimize for logo count, broad vertical coverage, or cold-dial volume before the product has real customer outcomes.

## Best first-partner profile

Prefer owner-operated or small-team businesses where one conversation can clearly move work forward:

- home services;
- local professional services;
- real estate teams;
- appointment-based businesses;
- agencies or service businesses with visible inbound demand.

Choose a partner because the problem is measurable, not because the logo looks impressive.

## Seven-day proof loop

| Step | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Partner creates a real account and one focused voice agent |
| 2 | Agent is trained on the partner's real facts, rules, voice, and handoff posture |
| 3 | A private test call passes before live traffic |
| 4 | Partner activates a paid plan and claims or connects the live number |
| 5 | One real inbound call is handled, or a consented non-marketing outbound call is completed where permitted |
| 6 | The same workflow continues by text, email, booking, approval, or human handoff when useful |
| 7 | The outcome and transcript/log are visible and the partner reviews what should improve |

A booked appointment is useful proof when the workflow calls for one. It is not the only valid outcome; qualified demand, resolved service, a clean handoff, or completed follow-up can prove value too.

## Launch constraints

- Federal marketing DNC remains fail-closed until the platform credential is configured. **Cold marketing outbound is not a prerequisite for the first design partners.**
- Demo/test activity remains labeled as demo/test and never becomes implied customer proof.
- Do not invent testimonials, ROI, adoption counts, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Use normal published plan economics unless a separate founder decision explicitly authorizes a temporary offer.
- Start with one job per partner. Add agents or workflow breadth only after the first loop works.

## Founder offer

Keep the offer simple:

> I built Wireline for calls and follow-up that fall through the cracks. I am onboarding a few design partners before a broad launch. I will personally help set up one voice agent around a real workflow, test it with you, and improve it from the outcomes. If you have one missed-call or follow-up problem worth fixing, I would like to run that workflow with you.

No fake urgency. No claim that other customers already use it.

## Success review

For each partner, record:

- time from signup to passing test call;
- time from activation to first live useful outcome;
- whether follow-up or handoff completed correctly;
- where the owner needed help;
- what the agent got wrong or needed retraining on;
- whether the partner would keep the workflow live at the published price;
- the exact product change, if any, that would make the workflow materially better.

Use server product events, call/message records, outcome logs, and the partner review itself as evidence. Do not promote an internal metric to a public claim until real customer evidence supports it.

## Exit criteria

Move from founder-led design partners to broader acquisition only when multiple real partners can complete the core loop without bespoke engineering and at least one repeatable problem/segment is clear.

Until then, depth beats volume.
