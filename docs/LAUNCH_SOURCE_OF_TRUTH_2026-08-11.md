# Wireline Launch Source of Truth

Date: 2026-08-11  
Status: **Active launch authority**  
Scope: public positioning, launch creative, dogfood proof, and pre-customer GTM

This document is the tie-breaker for active launch language and creative status. Historical audits remain historical. The campaign pack and creative media kit still own detailed scripts, shot lists, and production mechanics, but when their high-level positioning or asset-status language conflicts with this file, this file wins.

## One buyer story

**Headline:** Voice agents that get the work done.  
**Operating loop:** **Build -> Train -> Launch -> Improve**  
**Primary CTA:** Build your voice agent  
**Offer:** Build free. Go live from $19/mo.  
**Channel proof:** Calls, texts, email, follow-up, and logged outcomes.  
**Audience:** Personal and business use without making either audience secondary.

The public story has one operating loop. Do not rotate among `Brief -> Train -> Launch -> Improve`, `Create -> Train -> Wire -> Run`, and `Build -> Train -> Launch -> Improve` as competing brand spines.

### Product language versus technical architecture

The repository can keep technical surface names such as Create, Training, Connections, Phone, Inbox, and Studio. `Wire` remains a useful internal verb for integrations, numbers, routing, approvals, and launch controls. Those implementation nouns do **not** create a second public launch narrative.

Publicly:

1. **Build** the agent and give it a job.
2. **Train** it on facts, rules, voice, and behavior.
3. **Launch** it on the channels the user enables.
4. **Improve** it from real outcomes and review.

## Positioning guardrails

Use concrete operating language. Show the finished work, not abstract AI capability.

Use:

- Voice agents
- Personal or business
- Calls, texts, email
- Follow-up and logged outcomes
- Paid plans include a Wireline number
- Private simulation before live launch
- Start with one agent; add specialists when useful

Avoid on broad public surfaces:

- AI employee as the primary noun
- one-employee promises
- fully autonomous
- guaranteed bookings or revenue
- customer-count, ROI, or testimonial claims that do not yet have real customer evidence
- public WhatsApp claims while the feature remains outside the launch posture
- multiple workflow spines competing on the same journey

## Pre-customer proof standard

Wireline does not need fake social proof. Until real customers exist, launch proof comes from the product itself:

- a real agent answering a real test call;
- a real follow-up text or email;
- a real booking or handoff when configured;
- the real outcome/log appearing in Wireline;
- transparent demo/test labeling where context could otherwise imply customer production usage;
- exact-head screenshots and recordings from the production build being promoted.

Never manufacture customer logos, quotes, usage counts, revenue claims, or implied adoption.

## Publish gate for every reel

A reel is publishable only when all of the following are true:

- product picture was captured from the exact production source commit being promoted;
- no 404, sign-in wall, loading hold, error screen, or dead route appears in the edit;
- active UI remains legible after 9:16 or 1:1 reframing;
- no vendor marks or fabricated product UI are shown;
- burned-in captions are omitted unless a specific brief intentionally calls for sparse on-screen copy;
- selectable platform captions are provided for accessibility;
- at least one meaningful stretch lets the actual agent voice be heard clearly when the cut is selling voice quality;
- claims match current pricing, channel availability, and compliance posture;
- the end frame is deliberate and readable rather than an arbitrary route transition;
- the release record identifies the exact source commit.

A clean edit of stale footage is still stale footage. Provenance beats polish.

## Three launch cuts

### 1. Missed Call — 15 seconds

Goal: make an owner feel the cost of not being able to pick up, then show Wireline taking over.

Structure:

- 0-2s: recognizable missed-call moment; no product-name-first opening.
- 2-6s: Wireline agent / live-call proof.
- 6-11s: follow-up or outcome proof.
- 11-15s: clean product/brand hold and CTA.

No persistent subtitles over product UI.

### 2. Product Proof — 20-30 seconds

Goal: show the operating loop with almost no explanation.

Structure:

- Build
- Train
- Launch
- Improve
- CTA

Favor motion that proves a state change: configured agent, connected channel, live action, logged result.

### 3. Founder Walkthrough — 45-60 seconds

Goal: show why the product exists and why one trained agent spanning channels is more useful than disconnected tools.

Keep the founder narration practical. The picture should progress through real product states and end on the strongest clean Wireline frame. Do not fill time with route loading, cursor wandering, or repeated dashboards.

## Pre-customer dogfood loop

Until customer telemetry exists, the founder account and explicit demo/test accounts are the launch laboratory. Every launch pass should test:

- new-user signup and onboarding;
- agent creation from blank state;
- training/knowledge ingestion;
- phone connection or provisioning posture;
- inbound call;
- outbound call where compliance permits;
- SMS and email continuation;
- approval/handoff;
- booking/calendar action when connected;
- outcome and transcript/log visibility;
- billing activation, cancellation, and entitlement changes;
- mobile and desktop layout at the public and authenticated boundaries;
- zero-customer empty states as carefully as seeded states.

Dogfood evidence must stay clearly separated from customer evidence.

## First-customer launch motion

The immediate GTM objective is not to imply scale. It is to create enough credible product proof to recruit the first design partners.

1. Publish the strongest source-current founder/product reel.
2. Use the 15-second cut for targeted owner/operator acquisition once its current-source picture and real agent-audio proof are certified.
3. Drive every generic acquisition path to Free Builder first; paid intent can enter through Pricing.
4. Personally recruit a small number of design partners whose missed-call or follow-up problem is obvious and measurable.
5. Capture structured feedback and real outcomes before expanding testimonial, ROI, or vertical-specific claims.
6. Treat the first successful live conversations as product evidence, not as permission to inflate adoption claims.
