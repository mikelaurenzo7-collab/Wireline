# BotBound Design and Surface Audit

Date: 26 November 2026. Baseline: `main` at the start of the pass. Scope: the design system, then every signed-in surface (Bot, Inbox email and texts, Calls, Calendar, People, Knowledge, Insights, Connections, Computer, Settings) on its own and as one product.

Method: a seeded local account on a throwaway database, all 19 workspace routes at 1440px and 390px in light and dark, then focused flows across channels. No route overflowed horizontally and none logged a console error. Production data and providers were not touched.

## Score

72 of 100 before this pass. The token pipeline, atoms and responsive shell are sound. The gaps were in how surfaces connect, light-theme borders, and inconsistent formatting of phones, dates and headers.

## Fixed in this pass

## Final pass

A second pass the same day, desktop and phone in light and dark, took the score from 81 to 88.

| Area            | Finding                                                                                                                       | Resolution                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Page headers    | Knowledge showed a small title, Connections a large one, Approvals a display title.                                           | `WorkspacePageIntro size="compact"` puts the title in the shared control band, at the height and rule of the tab band on Calls, Calendar, Insights and Settings. Pages without tabs all use it. |
| Dates           | Calls, Texts and Email each had a local formatter; Compliance printed `11/23/2026, 10:00:00 AM`; Approvals added a zone name. | `formatActivityTime` in `safe-date.ts` ("Nov 26, 9:59 AM", year only outside the current year) on every channel. Calendar uses People's `appointmentWhen`, which now keeps rolled-over dates.   |
| Email           | The Email list showed bare addresses for saved customers.                                                                     | `/api/emails/threads` is labelled by `withPersonLabels(..., "email", ...)`; rows and the header show the name with the address.                                                                 |
| Calls, Outbound | New Call wrapped to its own band; a narrow card titled "Call" sat above a full-width disclosure.                              | One centered column: a "Place a Call" card holding New Call, then Recipient Permissions at the same width.                                                                                      |
| Calendar, phone | Availability text and cards touched the screen edge.                                                                          | Base horizontal padding.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Naming and case | The setup step said "Train" and "Open training" for Knowledge; sentence-case buttons sat beside Title Case ones.              | "Knowledge" and "Open Knowledge"; Title Case for Calendar, setup, Connections, Knowledge and Outbound labels.                                                                                   |
| Tokens          | 72 arbitrary text sizes, 64 arbitrary radii, 96 arbitrary letter-spacings in TSX.                                             | Product surfaces use the caption and overline tokens, the radius scale and the tracking scale. 10px labels are now the 11px overline size.                                                      |
| Controls        | Export All Calls used wide-tracked label styling; Delete Account had a boxed chevron.                                         | Standard secondary button; plain chevron.                                                                                                                                                       |
| Demo data       | Demo email bodies reused call summaries.                                                                                      | Email-shaped subjects, digests, messages and unsigned replies; existing demo threads are rewritten on reseed.                                                                                   |
