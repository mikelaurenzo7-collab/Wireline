# BotBound Design and Surface Audit

Date: 26 November 2026. Baseline: `main` at the start of the pass. Scope: the design system, then every signed-in surface (Bot, Inbox email and texts, Calls, Calendar, People, Knowledge, Insights, Connections, Computer, Settings) on its own and as one product.

Method: a seeded local account on a throwaway database, all 19 workspace routes at 1440px and 390px in light and dark, then focused flows across channels. No route overflowed horizontally and none logged a console error. Production data and providers were not touched.

## Score

72 of 100 before this pass. The token pipeline, atoms and responsive shell are sound. The gaps were in how surfaces connect, light-theme borders, and inconsistent formatting of phones, dates and headers.

## Final pass

A second pass the same day, desktop and phone in light and dark, took the score from 81 to 88.

## Launch pass

A third pass the same evening, after two independent code reviews of pull requests 1538 and 1539, a signed-in dogfood pass on production (Studio chat, Knowledge, Calendar, People), and a third review of this pass before release.

| Area      | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Resolution                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Texting   | Every read of the carrier registration failed with "Page size must be between 1 and 50": the SDK sends `limit` as the page size. Lines could never tell what the approved campaign covers.                                                                                                                                                                                        | Every list asks for pages of 50 (pull request 1540).                                                                                                                               |
| Texting   | That fix passed one options object to three carrier lists that run at the same time. The SDK writes each call's handlers onto the object it is given, so the read never finished: the first US text in a process would have waited forever, with every later send and the job worker behind it. The third review caught it; no text had been sent in the three hours it was live. | Each list gets its own options. The wait is capped at five seconds, after which the carrier decides; a late read still fills the cache. A test runs the read through the real SDK. |
| Calls     | A booking request still waiting for the owner read "Booked".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Only Scheduled, Confirmed, Rescheduled and Completed count.                                                                                                                        |
| Calls     | A People link to a call outside the loaded pages left the newest call, another customer's, in the report pane. The `call` parameter stayed in the URL and reopened that call on every tab change.                                                                                                                                                                                 | The list says the call is not in it, waits before auto-selecting, keeps a call the owner opened, and clears the parameter.                                                         |
| Calls     | A list that failed to load counted as "call not found" and removed the link from the URL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | The link waits for the next load.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Calls     | The five annotation reads for one page each took a pooled connection: the whole five-connection pool.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | One transaction, read in order.                                                                                                                                                    |
| People    | The chip offered "Save to People" for values that cannot be saved. The lookup put the customer's phone or email in the URL. The found-person memory was shared across accounts in one tab.                                                                                                                                                                                        | The server's own normaliser, a POST lookup, and a per-account key.                                                                                                                 |
| People    | The carrier's five stand-in numbers for a caller who withheld theirs counted as real phones. Saving one would have joined every anonymous caller into one person.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Refused as identifiers, in the chip, the suggestions and a save.                                                                                                                   |
| Admin     | `/admin` rendered for every account.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Platform admins only (owner address or `is_admin`). Every other account lands on the matching Settings page.                                                                       |
| Visual QA | The production gate still expected the Admin console for its capture account, an ordinary workspace.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | It expects the owner page each Admin link lands on. A test keeps the gate's table equal to the app's.                                                                              |
| Brand     | Business Brand defaulted to "BotBound" and saved it with any other change, which replaced the business name in texts and email.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Empty until set. The business name is the fallback.                                                                                                                                |
| Bots      | A Bot drafted from a connected app or the mobile app started in UTC.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | It starts in the zone most of the owner's Bots use.                                                                                                                                |
| Wording   | Vendor names reached owners and callers through send failures, two call summaries, refund reasons and the phone voice picker. "Training" still named Knowledge in Settings, Apps, and the connected-app tool description and consent page named the toggle by its old label.                                                                                                      | Plain wording at each boundary.
