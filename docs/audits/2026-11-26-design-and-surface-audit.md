# BotBound Design and Surface Audit

Date: 26 November 2026. Baseline: `main` at the start of the pass. Scope: the design system, then every signed-in surface (Bot, Inbox email and texts, Calls, Calendar, People, Knowledge, Insights, Connections, Computer, Settings) on its own and as one product.

Method: a seeded local account on a throwaway database, all 19 workspace routes at 1440px and 390px in light and dark, then focused flows across channels. No route overflowed horizontally and none logged a console error. Production data and providers were not touched.

## Score

72 of 100 before this pass. The token pipeline, atoms and responsive shell are sound. The gaps were in how surfaces connect, light-theme borders, and inconsistent formatting of phones, dates and headers.
