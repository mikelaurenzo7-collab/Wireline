## Current Creative Doctrine

Wireline's public creative promise is **AI Voice Agents for personal and business conversations**. The market should immediately understand that Wireline can start with one useful agent and grow into a coordinated roster, without making personal plans feel like an afterthought.

Use:

- **AI Voice Agents**
- **Build your AI Voice Agent**
- **Build free. Go live from $19/mo.**
- **Personal + Business**
- **Voice + SMS + Email**
- **Create -> Train -> Wire -> Run**

Do not use:

- broad AI-employee hero copy
- one-employee public promises
- old best-employee slogans
- sub-$19 launch pricing

Business-context assets may use "AI employee" only when the placement is explicitly Business workspace or Business plan oriented.

## Brand System

| Element | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | Cool near-white or cool obsidian canvas, electric-violet action, platinum wire accent, restrained status colors |
| Mood | Premium, clear, operational, outcome-driven |
| Imagery | Concrete product scenes: call card, SMS follow-up, email draft, outcome log, approval/handoff |
| Typography | Crisp SaaS, high contrast, no novelty effects |
| Avoid | Purple blobs, beige/tan palettes, abstract bokeh, fake humanoid robots, generic call-center stock |

## Plugin-Ready Design Briefs

### Canva / Adobe Express Static Ad

Prompt: Create a premium SaaS launch ad for Wireline. Headline: "AI Voice Agents". Subhead: "Personal + Business. Voice + SMS + Email." CTA: "Build free. Go live from $19/mo." Use a clean product-interface composition with an incoming call, SMS confirmation, and logged outcome. Use electric-violet accents, cool near-white or obsidian canvas, platinum wire detail, crisp type, and no generic stock photos.

### Adobe Firefly Product Scene

Prompt: A realistic premium SaaS workspace scene showing a laptop with a clean voice-agent dashboard, incoming call card, SMS follow-up, and outcome log. Modern founder/operator desk, subtle platinum wire accent, electric-violet UI highlight, professional lighting, no visible brand names except Wireline if text is supported.

### HeyGen / HyperFrames Founder Video

Prompt: Produce a 60-second founder-style launch video for Wireline. Tone: confident, warm, practical. Message: missed calls are high-intent moments; Wireline lets people and teams build AI voice agents for personal tasks and business conversations; start free, train privately, go live from $19/mo. End card: Build your AI Voice Agent at trywireline.com.

### BlitzReels Short Cut

Prompt: Cut a 15-second vertical ad around this sequence: missed call -> Wireline agent trained on rules -> call answered -> follow-up text sent -> outcome logged. Overlay copy: "Missed calls should not become lost revenue", "AI Voice Agents", "Build free. Go live from $19/mo."

### Beautiful.ai Deck

Prompt: Build a concise 6-slide launch deck for Wireline: 1) AI Voice Agents, 2) Missed calls are expensive, 3) Create -> Train -> Wire -> Run, 4) Personal + business use cases, 5) Founder Early Access from $19/mo, 6) Build your AI Voice Agent.
