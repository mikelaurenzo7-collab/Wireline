## Launch Killers To Clear First

1. **Phone loop:** inbound call, outbound call, SMS follow-up, outcome log, handoff.
2. **Billing loop:** checkout, entitlement, Customer Portal, cancel, Conversation Balance behavior.
3. **Copy drift:** no broad "one AI employee"; no "$9" launch promise; no public WhatsApp claim.
4. **Compliance:** consent, STOP handling, DNC posture, and outbound safeguards reviewed.
5. **Runtime:** production deploy READY; runtime errors and fatal logs clean.
6. **Secret hygiene:** revoke/rotate any personal tokens shared outside approved secret stores.

## Recommended Launch Sequence

### Day 0-1 - Verify

- Run smoke checks for public pages, signup, pricing, and checkout.
- Manually complete phone and payment loops.
- Confirm campaign docs and ads use AI Voice Agents and $19 floor.
- Confirm Vercel production logs are clean.

### Day 2-4 - Private Operator Trials

- Invite 5-10 early operators across personal, solo, and small-business use cases.
- Give each one a concrete first outcome: missed-call recovery, appointment follow-up, personal call delegation, support triage, or front desk coverage.
- Review every outcome log and capture confusion in onboarding/copy.

### Day 5-7 - Warm Launch

- Publish founder post, demo caption, and one product walkthrough.
- Use the AI Voice Generator ad audio only as a marketing VO/reference.
- Keep budget minimal until trial calls and checkout behavior are verified by hand.

### Day 8-14 - Paid Test

- Start with one campaign: `wl_launch_ai_voice_agents_v6`.
- Direct to signup and pricing pages.
- Pause any ad set that creates low-intent traffic or compliance ambiguity.
- Expand only after at least 14 clean ledger/webhook days and evidence of successful outcomes.
