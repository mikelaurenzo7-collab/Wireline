## Economics

Voice reserve $0.128/minute; one local number $1.15/month where Calls or SMS is selected; one managed mailbox $2/month where Email is selected. Model all included managed and transport allowances independently. Commercial fees are 4.1% plus $0.30 per actual payment; annual fixed fee is spread over twelve months. Full-use contribution must reach 30%, expected-use contribution 45% at 70% ordinary variable utilization; fixed number/mailbox capacity stays fully reserved. Each Calls package also reserves the full cost of one unpaid 10-minute completion buffer ($1.28 normal; $1.40 stress), without probability discounting. Stress $0.14/minute must retain at least 25% full / 40% expected.

Light / Regular / High each include one Bot with customizable roles. Higher capacity increases the account's usage allowances, not its Bot count or concurrency. Calls/SMS include one local number and Email includes one mailbox per account. Each connected identity is assigned to the Bot. One unsettled call is permitted per tenant. Extra-Bot add-ons for new channel packages are not available yet.

Light / Regular / High Calls include 50 / 250 / 500 minutes monthly. Standalone managed allowances are Calls $1.50 / $3 / $5; Email $2.50 / $10 / $30; SMS $2.50 / $7 / $15. Combinations add only each channel's incremental allowance above the Calls/base amount, so Email+SMS and all three receive $3.50 / $14 / $40. SMS transport is $2 / $10 / $30, separately funded. Annual prices are explicit, not ten times monthly; savings vary by package and capacity. Daily Calls guardrails are 60 / 250 / 500 minutes, with the same durable wallet admission. Channel-plan call minutes and managed AI allowances reset on the paid monthly subscription anniversary. Annual plans receive monthly slices anchored to the paid annual period, with month-end dates clamped without drift. Billing and Activity display the server-provided next reset date; unused included usage does not roll over. Legacy plans and Free Builder retain UTC calendar-month accounting. Missing verified paid-period evidence pauses new managed work until the subscription is refreshed in Billing; a calendar boundary never grants a second funded allowance.

Calls starts at $19/month or $199/year; Calls + Email is $25/$259, Calls + SMS $24/$249 and all three $29/$309. The latter two remain unavailable pending SMS readiness. Compared with a fully prepaid quote, these prices explicitly fund the bounded completion exposure. A new call must have at least one funded minute. Its allowed duration is funded minutes plus at most ten extra minutes, limited by the existing session ceiling. Admission stores one durable wallet hold and deadline per tenant; retries cannot reset that deadline. Settlement returns unused held funds or records the actual remaining charge. Debt pauses new calls and prepaid extras until paid; included Email/AI allowance and dashboard access continue. No automatic card charge or invoice is created. Confirmed unanswered or startup failures without Bot conversation are absorbed by the platform; ambiguous provider outcomes retain their hold until reconciled.

Each included phone slot permits one new carrier acquisition in a rolling 31-day window. Release, compensation and reassignment do not reset the window. Reassigning an existing line is free of a new acquisition; only definite provider rejection refunds an attempted purchase. This prevents repeated replacements from multiplying the modeled monthly number rental. See the [billing operations note](P0_BILLING_OPS_NOTE.md) for recovery semantics.

SMS rows exclude unknown per-tenant A2P charges and unbounded external incoming traffic. They are modeled quotes, with checkout disabled, not a claim of ready-to-sell SMS economics. Paid Lookup uses its own atomic reservation against managed provider allowance. The current outgoing-only transport debit is insufficient proof for receiving costs; completing that policy is mandatory before enabling SMS packages.

## Scope and compatibility

The user reports no real customers yet; existing accounts are demo/test. Retain historical IDs and allowance semantics for records and webhook replay, without treating old offers as available to new buyers. No automatic subscription migration, resource deletion or historical invoice rewrite. New channel plans include one Bot and do not use the historical extra-Bot bundle.

The managed Stripe portal remains payment/invoice/contact/cancellation-only. The in-app capacity endpoint supports only a higher usage capacity with identical channels and billing interval, scheduled at the next successful paid renewal (including the next annual renewal for annual customers). Higher account allowances start only after that paid renewal; Bot, phone and mailbox limits do not increase. There is no immediate proration, quota reset, channel switch or cancellation/re-purchase. Runtime top-ups remain the immediate option. The authoritative paid base-plan invoice must match the subscription, customer, target price and effective period before higher capacity is projected. A Stripe phase change alone is insufficient. Failed payments keep the old capacity and ordinary subscription payment gates still apply.

GET/POST/DELETE `/api/billing/capacity-upgrade` provide status, scheduling and removal. The operation is serialized with deletion cleanup; durable provider metadata and stable per-generation Stripe keys recover interrupted attempts. An incomplete attempt is labelled unconfirmed, not scheduled. Removing a change releases only the exact owned schedule and keeps the current subscription. Stripe portal cancellation is unavailable while any schedule is attached, so the UI exposes removal before cancellation; a paid applied phase is detached automatically without reverting its price. Foreign schedules are never overwritten. No automatic usage invoice or extra card charge is introduced.

Actual Stripe products and Render environment IDs remain a separately verified release step. Setup creates no SMS prices and preserves historical objects. Stripe Tax remains off until actual registrations and the existing opt-in are verified; the cost model still reserves the 0.5% transaction component.
