# AI Profit Agent Wedge — 2026-07-07

**Status:** Active wedge strategy.  
**Updated:** 2026-07-10 profit-agent positioning and included-number promise.

## Decision

Wireline should lead with **AI profit agents** without becoming outbound-only.

The marketable wedge is sharper than “AI phone employees” or “revenue agents”:

> **AI profit agents that actually call.**

The launch promise is:

> **Your best salesperson, without the commission.**

Use that as a punchy hook, then immediately ground it in truthful operating jobs: appointment setting, lead revival, offer promotion, quote follow-up, payment recovery, inbound conversion, follow-up, and outcome logging.

## Commercial promise

Every included Wireline agent should include one dedicated number. A number makes the agent feel real: it can call out, receive callbacks, be saved by customers, appear in routing, and become an operating asset instead of a software-only card.

The core wedge loop becomes:

1. **List** — who to call, why they are being called, consent/source, priority, attempt status.
2. **Pitch** — opener, offer, qualification, objections, compliance language, and escalation rules.
3. **Call** — schedule, number, cadence, live call, voicemail, retries, and handoff.
4. **Profit** — booked appointment, revived lead, promoted offer, recovered payment promise, callback, escalation, or clean next action.

For lower-risk product copy, use:

> **List → Script → Call → Outcome**

For marketing, the category should be:

> **AI Profit Agents**

## Why profit is stronger than revenue

### 1. Profit is owner-brain language

Revenue sounds like a SaaS dashboard. Profit sounds like the thing the business owner wants: a system that pays for itself.

### 2. Profit covers creation, recovery, and protection

Wireline should not only talk about new revenue. Profit includes:

- **Money made:** booked appointments, qualified leads, promotions, upsells, renewals.
- **Money recovered:** old leads, abandoned quotes, overdue accounts, dormant customers.
- **Money protected:** no-show recovery, missed-call capture, faster follow-up, fewer dropped opportunities.
- **Money saved:** less manual chasing, less payroll pressure, less commission drag.

### 3. Profit keeps the wedge broader than SDR

Avoid leading with “AI SDR” only. That is crowded and too narrow. Wireline should own the broader primitive: phone work that moves profit.

### 4. A free/included number makes the promise tangible

A buyer understands “my agent has its own number” faster than they understand “workflow orchestration.” The number is the physical-feeling anchor for the profit agent.

| Outbound | List → Pitch → Call → Profit | appointment setting, lead revival, quote follow-up, offer promotion, payment recovery, surveys, renewals | queued list row becomes booked, promised, recovered, callback, escalated, or no-answer |

## Positioning

### Primary line

**AI profit agents that actually call.**

### Secondary line

Wireline turns lists, missed calls, stale leads, promotions, and follow-up work into booked appointments, revived opportunities, payment promises, callbacks, and clean next steps.

### Economic hook

**No commission. No extra salesperson. Every included agent gets an included number.**

### What to avoid

Avoid implying guaranteed profit. Use “profit agents” as a category and intent, then prove it through concrete outcomes.

Avoid leading with “AI SDR” only. It invites crowded sales-tool comparisons and hides the appointment, promotion, recovery, and inbound conversion story.

Avoid leading with “phone agents for everything.” It is true, but too broad for launch.

Avoid implying fully autonomous revenue without controls. Profit agents require consent, call windows, approved scripts, escalation rules, and visible outcome logging.

## Product doctrine

Profit-agent surfaces should organize around the wedge loop:

1. **List** — who to call, why they are being called, consent/source, priority, attempt status.
2. **Pitch / Script** — opener, qualification, objections, compliance language, approved offers, escalation rules.
3. **Call** — schedule, number, cadence, live call, voicemail, retries, hold handling.
4. **Outcome / Profit** — outcome, summary, CRM update, payment promise, meeting booked, callback, escalation.

Inbound and hybrid should map to the same underlying operating system, not a separate product:

- Inbound: answer, qualify, book, log.
- Hybrid: answer, act, follow up, sync.

This keeps the platform unified: every mode has a clear input, a trained behavior, a reachable number, a live call, and an outcome record.

## Feature implications

### Must be first-class

- Direction choice: outbound, inbound, hybrid.
- Profit-agent role choice: appointments, lead revival, promotions, quote follow-up, recovery, inbound conversion.
- One included number per included agent.
- Import / create call lists for outbound lanes.
- Inbound routing and answer rules.
- Campaign or list view with call status.
- Approved scripts and objection handling.
- Compliance settings by lane.
- Call windows and retry cadence.
- Outcome taxonomy.
- CRM or workspace logging.
- Human handoff / approval rules.
- Payment link or meeting booking where authorized.

### Should be careful

- Collections and negotiation need explicit compliance disclaimers, permissioning, call windows, identity verification, and “never say” boundaries.
- Sales calls need clear source/consent metadata, do-not-call posture, and human escalation when the contact asks for details the agent cannot answer.
- Personal bill negotiation must never impersonate the user. The agent should openly represent itself as calling on behalf of the user.
- Inbound support and healthcare-adjacent scheduling need strong escalation rules and no unsupported legal, financial, or medical advice.
- “Profit” should never be phrased as a guarantee.

### Can wait

- Avatar/presenter marketing for call agents is secondary.
- Browser extension is a useful command surface, but not the wedge.
- Broad “call center for everything” messaging can wait until profit-agent proof and hybrid workflows are strong.

## Demo path

The best demo is not a generic chat.

1. Choose a profit role: appointment setter, lead revival, promotion, quote follow-up, recovery, or inbound conversion.
2. Show the agent’s included number.
3. Upload or create a list.
4. Show the script, answer rules, handoff rules, and guardrails.
5. Dial or answer one test call.
6. Show transcript, summary, outcome tag, and next action.
7. Show the list, inbox, CRM, or workspace updated in real time.

The magic moment is not the voice. The magic moment is the outcome row changing from **Queued** to **Booked**, **Promised**, **Recovered**, **Revived**, **Interested**, **Callback**, or **Escalated**.
