# Voice Cloning Expansion

**Status:** Superseded on 2026-07-11.

The earlier plan treated additional cloning vendors and a voice picker as the expansion strategy. That framing is no longer sufficient because cloning and realtime voice have become commodity platform capabilities.

The active source of truth is [VOICE_IDENTITY_MOAT_AND_PARTNERS_2026.md](./VOICE_IDENTITY_MOAT_AND_PARTNERS_2026.md), which reframes the product around:

- authorized and revocable Voice Identity,
- cross-provider portability,
- durable consent and deletion evidence,
- provider-neutral behavior and workflow continuity,
- benchmark-driven runtime routing,
- and verified business outcomes.

Cartesia remains a candidate secondary TTS/cloning provider, but only after controlled quality, latency, cost, privacy, and deletion benchmarking. ElevenLabs remains the current cloning provider; Wireline must not make any vendor ID the customer's canonical identity.
