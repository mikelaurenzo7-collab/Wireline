# Wireline Launch Campaign Pack - 2026-07-26

Status: **Active launch asset**. Use this as the current creative and paid-traffic source until a
newer campaign pack is promoted in `docs/README.md`.

## Campaign truth

Wireline is **your next AI employee for voice, text, and email conversations**. It is an agent
operating layer for business conversations, not a voice-bot marketplace.

Core proof loop: **Create -> Train -> Wire -> Run**.

Current release-status guardrails:

- The web operating loop is live.
- Browser Command, iOS, Android, and ChatGPT App surfaces are beta or gated unless a specific launch
  note says otherwise.
- Do not launch broad public ads until the live phone loop and payment loop are verified on the
  active production deployment.
- Do not invent metrics, logos, certifications, customer names, or guaranteed call-answer lift.
- Dedicated number and caller identity copy must stay truthful: availability and display depend on
  carrier, provider, registration, authentication, and coverage.

Retired price line: **Do not use "from $9" while Founder Early Access v6 is live.** Current public
entry point is Free Builder, then live from **$19/mo**.
