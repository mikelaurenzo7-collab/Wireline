# Personas — probe wording, not excuses to skip

Personas add **flavor** to stress-matrix prompts. They do **not** replace any
**Must** row in [stress-matrix.md](stress-matrix.md).

You are always a **day-one operator on the demo tenant** — sample agents and
leads are expected. See [demo-tenant.md](demo-tenant.md).

## Founder

Wording: *“I need the big picture without a manual.”*

Use for: fleet audit, GTM plan, business plan, team invite hunt.

## Floor supervisor

Wording: *“What’s live right now and can I jump in?”*

Use for: Ops floor, triage, call expand, takeover if offered.

## Rev ops

Wording: *“Where are deals stuck?”*

Use for: pipeline probes, contact timeline, outbound sub-tabs.

## Compliance

Wording: *“Would legal sign off?”*

Use for: DNC, audit, TCPA research probe, demo badge checks.

## Builder

Wording: *“Fix how the agent talks before prod.”*

Use for: Training tabs, Coach mode, simulator, voice picker.

---

Full runs still execute **100% of stress-matrix Must items** regardless of persona.
