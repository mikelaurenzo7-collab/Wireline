# Wireline Perfection Audit & Strategic Roadmap ## 1. Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 Compliance) **Status:** High-Fidelity UI, but requires structural semantic optimization. ### Observations: - **Contrast Ratios:** The Ametrine Purple (#5d3fd3) on white surface-bright (#f8f9ff) is excellent (4.6:1), meeting WCAG AA. However, interactive states on `surface-dim` should be monitored. - **Target Size:** Mobile buttons (Agent Controls) are generous, but text-based links like "Forgot password?" need a minimum 24x24px hit area per 2.5.8. - **Twin-View Pattern:** The complex "Knowledge Base" conflict resolution UI and "CRM Field Mapping" would benefit from a toggleable simplified table view for screen readers. ### perfection pass: - Ensure all SVG icons (2px stroke) have descriptive `aria-label` tags. - Implement 'Focus Not Obscured' logic for the sticky `TopAppBar`. --- ## 2. SEO & Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) **Status:** Transitioning from Keywords to Information Gain. ### Strategic Recommendations: - **JSON-LD Schema:** Define Wireline's AI agent capabilities (Build, Train, Run, Review) as structured data. This allows LLMs (Gemini, ChatGPT) to cite Wireline as a specific solution for "high-stakes AI communication." - **Latency Optimization:** Prioritize the 'Time to Interactive' for the embedded playground/chat widgets. 2026 ranking factors penalize heavy LLM script delays. - **Human-in-the-Loop Content:** Shift blog/marketing content toward unique case studies and "Trust & Data Sovereignty" whitepapers rather than generic "how-to" guides. --- ## 3. Marketing & Conversion (Premium SaaS) **Status:** Sophisticated visual style, needs Headline Specificity. ### Conversion Pass: - **TTV Headline:** Instead of "Never wait on hold again," test "Resolve 98% of inbound queries instantly." Specificity drives premium conversion. - **Trust Proximity:** Move the SOC2/Privacy guarantees directly adjacent to the "Start Free Trial" buttons on the landing and pricing pages. - **Outcome-Based Pricing:** Ensure the "Business" and "Enterprise" tiers clearly define the *usage value* (e.g., interactions handled) alongside the per-seat cost. --- ## 4. Branding Perfection (Ametrine Precision) **Status:** Highly Cohesive. ### Final Polish: - **'Glow' AI States:** Use the high-contrast violet accent *exclusively* for active agent states (e.g., the pulsing indicator in the Dynamic Island and Agent Studio). - **Variable Typography:** Leverage the full weight range of Hanken Grotesk for micro-hierarchies in dense dashboards like "Inbox & Activity." - **Skeuomorphic-Minimalism:** Maintain the subtle physical depth found in the App Icon across all primary card headers for a "tactile premium" feel.
