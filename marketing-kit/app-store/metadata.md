# Wireline App Store Metadata

## App name

Wireline: AI Call Assistant

## Subtitle

Never wait on hold again

## Short description / promotional text

Ask Wireline to make the calls, wait on hold, get the answer, and send you a clear recap.

## Long description

Wireline is your personal AI call and message assistant. It handles the routine phone work that interrupts your day: appointments, support lines, school or pharmacy calls, reservations, insurance follow-ups, callbacks, and the messages that need a clear next step.

Start with one assistant for your own life. Teach it your preferences, connect the accounts you approve, and let it keep track of the facts, requests, actions, and recaps around each job.

### What Wireline helps with

- Call businesses, support lines, offices, and service providers.
- Wait on hold so you do not have to.
- Ask the questions you provide.
- Capture confirmations, answers, and next steps.
- Send clear recaps and follow-up reminders.
- Keep useful knowledge and preferences in one place.
- Expand into business workflows only when you want that extra setup.

### Native experience

Wireline is built for the phone: mobile controls, widgets, notifications, accessibility, and native app surfaces are designed around quick review and consent. High-stakes decisions still come back to you.

### Important notes

Wireline does not replace emergency services, legal advice, medical advice, or financial advice. Some calling, messaging, account connection, and device features depend on carrier, platform, and connected-account availability.
