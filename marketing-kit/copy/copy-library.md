## Feature Copy Blocks

### Everyday Call Delegation

> Phone work is still everywhere: prescriptions, appointments, school offices, airlines, restaurants, landlords, utilities, and support lines. Wireline gives the agent a clear instruction, makes the call, waits when needed, and brings back the result.

### Agent Studio

> Studio is where a simple call concierge becomes yours. Give the agent context, hours, escalation rules, scripts, connected calendars, and follow-up behavior without splitting personal and business products.

### Never Fabricates Actions

> Wireline has a hard rule: the agent cannot claim it booked, changed, refunded, transferred, or submitted something unless it has the tool or explicit confirmation to do it. If it cannot act, it says so and captures the next step.
