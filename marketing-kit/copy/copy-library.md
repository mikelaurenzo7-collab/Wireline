## Feature Copy Blocks

### Never Fabricates Actions

> Every voice AI competitor has the same failure mode: the agent
> confidently tells the caller it booked the appointment, transferred
> the call, or processed the refund — then does nothing. The caller
> discovers the lie 24 hours later and never calls back.
>
> Wireline runs a hard architectural rule: an agent cannot claim an
> action it doesn't have a tool for. No tool for booking? It takes a
> message. No tool for refunds? It escalates to a human. Every one of
> these moments is visible in the live cockpit — you see what was
> handled and what needs you.

### Sub-Second Response Time

> Dead air kills calls. When a caller stops talking and hears silence
> for 2+ seconds, they assume the line dropped. Most voice AI platforms
> hit 1.5–2.5 seconds of silence — long enough to lose the caller.
>
> Wireline's voice stack (Claude Sonnet 4.6 + ElevenLabs Flash v2.5)
> delivers first audio in ~700–900 milliseconds. That's faster than
> most humans pause to think. The caller never wonders "are you there?"

### Supervisor Cockpit

> You wouldn't let a new hire take calls unsupervised on day one.
> Why would you let an AI?
>
> Wireline's supervisor cockpit shows every active call in real-time:
> live transcripts, caller sentiment, agent confidence scores. Pause
> the agent mid-sentence, inject a correction, or take over the call
> entirely. It's the training floor your AI fleet deserves.
