## Feature Copy Blocks

### Everyday Call Delegation

> Phone work is still everywhere: prescriptions, appointments, school offices, airlines, restaurants, landlords, utilities, and support lines. Wireline gives the assistant a clear instruction, makes the call, waits when needed, and brings back the result.

### Assistant Studio

> Assistant Studio is where a simple call concierge becomes yours. Give the assistant context, hours, escalation rules, scripts, connected calendars, and callback behavior without splitting personal and business products.

### Never Fabricates Actions

> Wireline has a hard rule: the assistant cannot claim it booked, changed, refunded, transferred, or submitted something unless it has the tool or explicit confirmation to do it. If it cannot act, it says so and captures the next step.
