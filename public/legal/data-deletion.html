Data Deletion

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Delete your account from the app

The fastest path is to sign in and open Settings > Account > Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm. Wireline immediately asks the billing provider to stop future subscription renewal, while paid access may remain available through the current billing period. Your account then enters a 30-day grace period. After that window, Wireline permanently purges tenant-owned product data, including call history, optional recordings, agent configuration, connected accounts, messages, lead records, and account-scoped audit events. Hard deletion is irreversible. During the grace period, choose Keep my account in Settings to cancel deletion. Wireline then asks the billing provider to restore renewal when applicable and tells you if billing still needs review.

Don't have access to your account?

Email support@trywireline.com from the address tied to the account with the subject line "Data deletion request". We verify the request before starting the same 30-day grace-period workflow and will confirm the scheduled deletion date.

What's deleted

  • User profile (email, name, password hash)
  • All agent configurations + uploaded knowledge documents
  • Call logs, transcripts, recordings, sentiment scores
  • Email threads, SMS threads, draft messages
  • OAuth connections + encrypted access tokens
  • Meta Lead Ads submissions ingested via webhook
  • Apollo lead enrichment cache scoped to your account
  • Billing history beyond what tax law requires us to retain

What's retained

Wireline may retain only information required for legal, fraud-prevention, security, or financial-record obligations, and only for the applicable retention period. Payment processors and financial institutions may retain transaction records under their own legal obligations. Production backups age out on their normal retention schedule and are not restored for ordinary product use after deletion.

Questions

Reach us at support@trywireline.com with any data deletion question. We aim to respond within two business days.
