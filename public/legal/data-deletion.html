Data Deletion

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Delete your account from the app

The fastest path is to sign in and head to Admin → Account → Delete Account. Confirm the deletion in the modal and your account enters a 30-day soft-delete window. After that window, every row tied to your user is purged from our database: call logs, recordings, agent configurations, OAuth connections (Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, Apollo, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Zoom, Meta), email accounts, lead records, billing history, audit trail. Hard delete is irreversible.

Don't have access to your account?

Email trywireline@yahoo.com from the address tied to the account with the subject line "Data deletion request". We verify the request out of band (text or call to a phone number on file) and then run the same deletion job manually. We confirm by email when the purge completes; SLA is 7 business days.

What's deleted

  • User profile (email, name, password hash)
  • All agent configurations + uploaded knowledge documents
  • Call logs, transcripts, recordings, sentiment scores
  • Email threads, SMS threads, draft messages
  • OAuth connections + encrypted access tokens
  • Meta Lead Ads submissions ingested via webhook
  • Apollo lead enrichment cache scoped to your account
  • Billing history beyond what tax law requires us to retain

What's retained

Anonymized, aggregated metrics (total calls per day across all tenants, no identifiers) used for capacity planning. Tax records required by US law (invoices, payment receipts) for the statutory retention window. Audit log entries with your user ID redacted to a stable hash for legitimate security investigation purposes.

Questions

Reach us at trywireline@yahoo.com with any data deletion question and we'll respond within 2 business days.
